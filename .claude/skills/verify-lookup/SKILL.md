# Verify lookup and title search

Read `../verify-app/common.md` first (setup, closing check, report). Never call the real worker or Goodreads: mock everything with `page.route`.

1. **Lookup (mocked).** `LOOKUP_URL` in `src/lookup.js` may be empty, so serve a copy: `page.route('**/src/lookup.js', …)` replacing `LOOKUP_URL = ''` with `'https://lookup.test'`; mock `https://lookup.test/**` (with `access-control-allow-origin`) and the cover host. Hold the answer for `9780747532699` behind a gate: card and Scanned row appear at once as `ISBN 9780747532699` with no `.cover`, and the status stays empty (a typed search shows no message); after the gate opens the heading and row show the found title and `.cover` appears (empty `alt`, loaded).
   - The answer also carries `series`, `author`, `rating`: the card shows `.rating` (gold ★, value with two decimals, count in brackets grouped like `734 385`) and a `.facts` block with `S.card.seriesLabel` (link to `https://www.goodreads.com/search?q=<name>`, then ` #<number>`) and `S.card.authorLabel` (link to the author's Goodreads page; plain text if the answer has no https author link). Missing parts are simply absent (no empty rows). No sideways scroll at 360 px, light and dark.
   - A typed name wins over the found title and survives a later lookup; clearing it falls back to the found title. A 404 leaves `ISBN <number>`. A reload shows the cached title and cover with no new lookup request.
   - While an answer is held, the card has a `.spinner` beside its title and every Scanned/Marked row of a book being looked up has one (search three ISBNs in a row: three rows spin, the card only for the shown one); each goes away on its own answer, 404 or failure. (Keep scanning with a held answer is in `verify-scan`, check 6.)
2. **Title search.** Mock `**/search?*` (two candidates with `rating`, `ratingCount`, `bookUrl`, `authorUrl`), `**/book?*` (`{isbn, title, …}`: the card must be complete with no `/lookup` call; `404 {}` for one), `**/lookup?*` (a title answer), shaped like `worker/README.md`. Type `Хірург Тесс` and press Search: status `S.search.pickBook`, two `.candidate` rows (cover, title, author, and a right-aligned `.candidate-rating`: `★ 4.29` from the mocked `rating`, `★ n/a` when it is missing or 0; no count, no thin mark; an answer without the new fields still works). The list hides, and the status clears with it, when the field text changes, on a click outside the list (blank space too), on Escape and on picking a candidate, but not on a click into the field. Picking a candidate shows its card at once (hold `/book` behind a gate): title, author, cover and `.rating` from the candidate, a row in Scanned and a working star, with no "getting ISBN" or "couldn't find the ISBN" message even when `/book` answers `404 {}` (that path has no dead end; those texts are gone from `strings.js`). Series is read from a title like `Dune (Dune, #1)`. Once `/book` answers, its details replace the partial ones (`isbn-reader.meta` entry loses `partial`, gains `isbn`; the ISBN is not shown). The card's links (`.link-button` and the row's `.icon-link`) are title searches (`ereadian.com/search?q=Хірург`: title only; `goodreads.com/search?q=Хірург Tess Gerritsen`: title and author, as the candidate gave them). The Goodreads link is the candidate's `bookUrl` (a title-and-author search when it has none); the same in the rows. `localStorage['isbn-reader.search']` holds the entry (`id` `gr:<id>`, `url`); a reload keeps the row, and clicking it shows the card again (a reload of the page does not, until deep links for these books exist). Typing the ISBN afterwards restores the ISBN links and empties `isbn-reader.search`. `1234567890123` is still the invalid-ISBN message; `1984` is searched as a title.
3. **Why a lookup failed (extension and `S.card.lookupWhy`).** The page shows no text for a failed lookup yet; the check is in `node` or the extension card. Answer `/lookup` with `404 {reason}` for each of `page-404`, `page-empty`, `page-refused`, `page-down`, `page-no-book` (with the "format changed" hint), `404 {}` (`no-reason`), a 500 (`error` with its code), a dropped network (`offline`), a slow answer (`timeout`): the extension card shows `S.card.lookupWhy` for each, in the same words.

Optional (online only): open `https://www.goodreads.com/book/isbn/9780747532699` and `https://ereadian.com/search?q=9786171299412` once to confirm they still lead somewhere sensible.

Finish with the closing check from `common.md`. The only console errors expected here are the browser's own "Failed to load resource" lines for the mocked 404/500 answers.

**Details version 6 / book id.** Details stored before version 6 (`v:5`) are asked again once, silently (no message, no spinner change beyond the usual), when the book's card is next built. A `/lookup` answer with `bookId` re-keys the book `isbn:X` → `gr:N` (see `verify-lists` 7c); a failed lookup leaves storage as it was and the next card build asks again.
